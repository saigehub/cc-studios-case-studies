Project Context M-DCPS · Your Best Choice Campaign

A school district on
defense.
A message worth delivering.

Miami-Dade County Public Schools entered 2024 managing a 13,000-student enrollment decline. The sharpest single-year drops in the district's modern history. The projected loss was 5,000 students. The actual loss was 13,059. The budget consequence was an immediate $100 million reduction.

Your Best Choice was the district's coordinated public response — a campaign to reframe M-DCPS as the competitive, premier educational choice in South Florida. CC Studios produced the visual content anchoring that campaign.

The Enrollment Reality
  • 326,279 students enrolled prior to decline
  • 313,220 students post-contraction — a loss of 13,059
  • District projected 5,000 losses. Actual overshoot: 8,000
  • $100M immediate budget reduction following the drop
  • Campaign positioned against private, charter, and voucher alternatives
